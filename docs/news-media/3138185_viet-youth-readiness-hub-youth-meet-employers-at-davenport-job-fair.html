Viet Youth Readiness Hub youth meet employers at Davenport job fair

April 26, 2026 · Toronto, Ontario

TORONTO, April 26, 2026 — Young people from the Viet Youth Readiness Hub (VYRH), a youth initiative of the Canada Vietnam Cultural & Educational Council (CVCEC), met employers at the Davenport Job Fair, hosted by the office of Davenport MP Julie Dzerowicz.

At the fair, participants:

  • met employers from across Toronto’s Davenport area and learned about current labour market needs;
  • explored career pathways in Canada, including a session with Toronto Fire Services on public service careers;
  • practised communication and networking skills in a professional setting.

VYRH focuses on practical, real-world experiences that help young people prepare for school and working life in Canada. CVCEC thanked the organizers for hosting the event.

About CVCEC
The Canada Vietnam Cultural & Educational Council (CVCEC) is a federally incorporated not-for-profit organization based in Toronto. It offers Vietnamese language and culture classes for children, helps newcomer and international students settle into Canadian schools and connects education communities in Canada and Vietnam. More information is available at cvcec.org.

About the Viet Youth Readiness Hub
Launched in Toronto in April 2026, the Viet Youth Readiness Hub (VYRH) is a CVCEC initiative that helps Vietnamese students and families understand Canadian schools through orientation sessions, mentoring and youth leadership opportunities.
