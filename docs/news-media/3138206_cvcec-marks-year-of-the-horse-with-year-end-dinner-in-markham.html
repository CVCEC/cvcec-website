CVCEC marks Year of the Horse with year-end dinner in Markham

Feb 6, 2026

TORONTO, Feb. 6, 2026 — The Canada Vietnam Cultural & Educational Council (CVCEC) hosted a year-end dinner for partners and friends at Sen Vietnamese Cuisine in Markham, Ont., ahead of the Lunar New Year and the Year of the Horse.

The gathering gave guests a chance to share stories, thank partners for their support and celebrate Vietnamese traditions while living in Canada.

CVCEC thanked Deon Nguyen and his wife, owners of Sen Vietnamese Cuisine, and their team for their hospitality.

Tea artisan Nguyen Ngoc Tuan, known as Lao Tra, also attended and shared stories about Vietnamese tea culture with guests.

CVCEC wished its partners and friends peace, prosperity and success in the Year of the Horse.

About CVCEC
The Canada Vietnam Cultural & Educational Council (CVCEC) is a federally incorporated not-for-profit organization based in Toronto. It offers Vietnamese language and culture classes for children, helps newcomer and international students settle into Canadian schools and connects education communities in Canada and Vietnam. More information is available at cvcec.org.
