Our programs

CVCEC runs community programs in Canada and helps connect education communities in Canada and Vietnam. Explore each program below.

Language & culture

Trường Tiếng Việt Của Em (My Vietnamese School)

A free online platform where Vietnamese-Canadian children learn Vietnamese language and culture at home with their families: self-paced lessons, interactive games, and writing, speaking and alphabet practice, designed by our teaching team in Ontario, Manitoba and Prince Edward Island.

Visit the learning platform →

Student readiness

Viet Youth Readiness Hub (VYRH)

Our youth initiative in Toronto, launched in April 2026. VYRH helps Vietnamese students prepare for and settle into Canadian schools through orientation sessions, mentoring and youth leadership opportunities.

Learn about VYRH →

For families

Study in Canada guide

A bilingual guide (Vietnamese and English) for families considering secondary school in Canada: Ontario’s public school system, the TDSB application pathway, the OSSD diploma, short videos and FAQs. Free of charge, no obligation.

Visit the Study in Canada guide →

Culture

Vietnamese Culture Day

A youth celebration of Vietnamese culture in Toronto, sharing áo dài, food and traditions with students of all backgrounds. Vietnamese Culture Day 2026 takes place on November 18 at Vega Academy, co-hosted with Pickle & Proof.
