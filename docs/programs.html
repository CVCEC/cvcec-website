School readiness

Helping newcomer and international students settle and succeed

Moving to a new school system is a big step. We give families accurate, practical information about how Ontario’s public schools work, before and after arrival.

  • Study in Canada guide: a free bilingual (Vietnamese–English) site on Ontario’s public school system, the OSSD diploma, the TDSB application pathway, short videos and FAQs
  • Orientation: sessions before and after arrival, including a free 45-minute course on Canadian classroom culture with our learning partner MapleSpark
  • Safety first: how to check that a study or immigration consultant is licensed, and how to avoid common scams

Visit duhoccanada.cvcec.org

Check a consultant on the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants public register. For official information about studying in Canada, visit EduCanada.ca.

Education partnerships

Connecting education communities in Canada and Vietnam

We act as a trusted connector between school boards, colleges, universities and families. We share knowledge about Canadian public education, support professional dialogue and help build long-term partnerships that respect local context.

  • Dialogue with the Toronto District School Board on Vietnamese language programs and student pathways
  • Sponsor of the global education roundtable at the 8th Ontario K-12 International Education Conference
  • Partnerships with colleges and universities in Canada and Vietnam
