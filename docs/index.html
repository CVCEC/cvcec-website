Education diplomacy, in practice

We connect families, schools and communities in Canada and Vietnam, so that Vietnamese-Canadian students can thrive here and educators in both countries can learn from each other.

A family’s story

When this family moved to Toronto in 2025, their daughter was starting Grade 10. Making friends with classmates from around the world was hard at first. She felt unsure of herself and was shy to speak up.

Through CVCEC, she joined a youth volunteer program with Toronto City Councillor Lily Cheng.

“Thank you. Our daughter has become so much more confident and so much braver.”

— Her parents (translated from Vietnamese)

How we work

Transparent, accountable and community-focused

CVCEC is a federally incorporated non-profit that connects students, families, educators and institutions in Canada and Vietnam. We work openly with school boards, colleges, universities and community organizations, and our programs follow Canadian non-profit governance and provincial curriculum guidelines. Our focus is simple: helping young people learn, belong and lead in Canada while keeping their Vietnamese language and culture alive.

Read about our governance →

Memberships and networks

CVCEC is a member of Canadian non-profit and community networks that strengthen our governance, learning and capacity to serve youth and families.

Our education partners

We work with public school boards, colleges and universities in Canada and Vietnam to advance education and cultural exchange. Our Viet Youth Readiness Hub was also selected for the Leading Urban Change program at the School of Cities, University of Toronto.
