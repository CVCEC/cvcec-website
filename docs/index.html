Cultures align · Knowledge shines

Young people building a bridge between Canada and Vietnam

Kết nối giáo dục & văn hoá Canada – Việt Nam, đồng hành cùng thế hệ trẻ.

CVCEC is a Toronto-based not-for-profit. We help Vietnamese-Canadian youth lead with confidence, give children of Vietnamese families abroad a free place to learn their language, and open the door to Vietnam for every Canadian who is curious about it.

Our role

A Canadian bridge to Vietnam

Rooted in Toronto and trusted in Vietnam, CVCEC connects families, young people, schools and universities on both sides, so language, knowledge and opportunity can move in both directions.
