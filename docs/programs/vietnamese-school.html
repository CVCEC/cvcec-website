Vietnamese language and culture, free for every learner

One part of CVCEC’s bridge between Canada and Vietnam: a free school for children of Vietnamese families living abroad, and for everyone who wants to understand Vietnam through its language.

Who it is for

Two kinds of learners, one open school

Children of Vietnamese families abroad

Dành cho con em kiều bào

Many Vietnamese-Canadian children understand Vietnamese at home but rarely use it anywhere else. The school gives them a clear learning path, from the alphabet to reading and writing, so they can talk with grandparents, read stories and feel proud of where their family comes from.

  • Lessons that follow Vietnam’s national curriculum, step by step
  • Games, worksheets and speaking practice to use at home
  • Community classes and a parent guide for families

Everyone curious about Vietnam

Dành cho bạn bè quốc tế

You do not need Vietnamese roots to learn here. Canadian classmates, teachers, partners and friends are welcome to start with the language, then explore Vietnam through folk tales, proverbs, songs and an interactive map.

  • Open to learners of Vietnamese as a second language
  • Folk tales, proverbs, songs and a 31-stop map journey across Vietnam
  • A friendly way to know your Vietnamese-Canadian neighbours

How you can learn

Online anywhere, together in community classes

Learners can study on their own at vietclass.cvcec.org, or join a community class led by CVCEC teachers. In-person classes, online classes and the free platform all follow one shared learning path, standardized by our Vietnamese Language & Academic Director, Ms. Hanna.

Questions

About the school

Does it cost anything?

No. The online school, the learning corners and our community classes are free for families.

My child only speaks a little Vietnamese. Where do we start?

Start with the lesson path at vietclass.cvcec.org. Lessons follow Vietnam’s national curriculum step by step, so children can begin at the level that fits them, and parents can follow along with the parent guide.

I am an adult with no Vietnamese background. Can I learn too?

Yes. The school is open to learners of Vietnamese as a second language. The folk tales, proverbs, songs and Discover Vietnam map are a good place to begin.

Can my school or community group start a class with CVCEC?

We are glad to talk with schools, school boards and community groups. Email contact@cvcec.org to start a conversation.

Teach, volunteer or bring a class to your community

Parents, teachers and volunteers keep the school going. We would love to hear from you.
