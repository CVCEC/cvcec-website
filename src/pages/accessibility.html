Accessibility

CVCEC is committed to making our website, programs and events accessible to everyone, in line with the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA).

Our website

  • We describe images with alternative text.
  • We use clear headings, plain language and readable colour contrast.
  • We keep links and buttons descriptive, so they make sense on their own.

Some content, such as older photos or documents from partners, may not yet meet every standard. We are working to improve it.

Our programs and events

If you or your child need an accommodation to take part in a program or event, please tell us in advance and we will do our best to help.

Other formats and feedback

If you have difficulty using our website, or need information in another format or in Vietnamese, contact us at contact@cvcec.org. We welcome your feedback and will respond as soon as we can.
