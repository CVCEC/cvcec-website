Governance and accountability

CVCEC is a federal not-for-profit corporation based in Toronto. We aim to be open about who we are, how we are run and how we use the support we receive.

Legal status

The Canada Vietnam Cultural & Educational Council (CVCEC) is incorporated under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act (Corporation No. 1657486-6) with Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada. We report on our activities and finances as required under federal rules.

How we are funded

We do not charge families for our programs. Our work is supported by partners, in-kind sponsors and volunteers, and any surplus is reinvested in community programs.

Independence

CVCEC is an independent Canadian organization. We work openly with school boards, community organizations and public institutions in Canada and Vietnam, and we do not act on behalf of any government. We do not share the personal information of students or families with any government body, except where required by Canadian law.

Land acknowledgement

CVCEC is based in Toronto. We acknowledge that the land we work on is the traditional territory of many nations including the Mississaugas of the Credit, the Anishnabeg, the Chippewa, the Haudenosaunee and the Wendat peoples, and is now home to many diverse First Nations, Inuit and Métis peoples. We also acknowledge that Toronto is covered by Treaty 13 with the Mississaugas of the Credit. As an organization that helps newcomer families make Canada home, we are committed to learning about Indigenous histories and sharing them with the students and families we serve.
