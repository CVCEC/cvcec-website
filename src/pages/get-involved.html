Get involved

There is a place for you at CVCEC

Parents, students, educators, businesses and volunteers all help Vietnamese-Canadian children and newcomer families thrive. Here is how you can take part.

Families and students

Join our free programs: online Vietnamese lessons for children, orientation for students coming to Canada, and youth leadership with the Viet Youth Readiness Hub.

Explore our programs

Volunteer

Help at community and cultural events, mentor newcomer students, support Vietnamese language learning, or share skills in design, media, translation and event planning.

Offer your time

Schools and organizations

We work with school boards, colleges, universities and community organizations to co-host information sessions, cultural days, language programs and youth activities. If you serve international or newcomer students, let’s talk.

Start a partnership

Local businesses

Businesses help our programs happen through in-kind support such as venues, food, printing and supplies. Sponsors are thanked in our news releases and at events.

Sponsor a program

Donate garments

Share a traditional garment

CVCEC continues to collect áo dài and other traditional garments for cultural showcases, workshops and photo experiences in Toronto. Donors are recognized as CVCEC cultural ambassadors.
