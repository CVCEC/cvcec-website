Get involved

There are many ways to support Vietnamese-Canadian children, newcomer students and their families. Whether you are a parent, a student, an educator, a business or a volunteer, we would love to work with you.

Families and students

Join a free program

Join our free programs: online Vietnamese lessons for children, orientation sessions for students coming to Canada, and youth leadership activities with the Viet Youth Readiness Hub.

Explore our programs →

Volunteer

Share your time and skills

Help at community and cultural events, mentor newcomer students, support Vietnamese language learning, or share professional skills such as design, media, translation and event planning.

Partner with us

Schools and organizations

We work with school boards, colleges, universities and community organizations to co-host information sessions, cultural days, language programs and youth activities. If your organization serves international or newcomer students, let’s talk.

Sponsor a program

Local businesses

Local businesses help our programs happen through in-kind support such as venues, food, printing and prizes.

Contact us

Email contact@cvcec.org and tell us how you would like to get involved. We will get back to you as soon as we can.
