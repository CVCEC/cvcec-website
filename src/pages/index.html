Rooted in Canada

A Canadian organization for a growing Canadian community

Vietnamese is one of the most widely spoken non-official languages in Canada: about 233,000 people spoke it at the time of the 2021 Census. Many children of Vietnamese-Canadian families understand Vietnamese at home but rarely get to use it anywhere else.

CVCEC was created in Toronto to fill that gap with a community-led approach. We follow Canadian non-profit governance, work openly with school boards and community organizations, and do not charge families for our programs.

  • Member of Imagine Canada, the Ontario Nonprofit Network and the Centre for Social Innovation
  • In dialogue with the Toronto District School Board on Vietnamese language programs
  • Selected for the Leading Urban Change program at the University of Toronto’s School of Cities

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Email contact@cvcec.org and we will get back to you.

Is CVCEC a not-for-profit organization?

Yes. CVCEC is incorporated under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act (Corporation No. 1657486-6) and is based in Toronto. It is governed by a board of directors and reinvests any surplus in community programs.

Do families pay for CVCEC programs?

No. We do not charge families for our programs. Our work is supported by partners, in-kind sponsors and volunteers.

Who can take part?

Vietnamese-Canadian children and families, newcomer and international students, educators, and anyone who wants to learn Vietnamese. Youth programs are run through the Viet Youth Readiness Hub in Toronto.

Where are your Vietnamese classes?

Our free online platform is open to learners anywhere. Community classes run in Prince Edward Island, an online class in Manitoba connects with PEI students, and a community class for young children started in Toronto in April 2026.

How can a school or organization partner with CVCEC?

We co-host information sessions, cultural days, language programs and youth activities with school boards, colleges, universities and community organizations. Email contact@cvcec.org to start a conversation.

Does CVCEC act for any government?

No. CVCEC is an independent Canadian organization. We work openly with public institutions in Canada and Vietnam, do not act on behalf of any government, and do not share students’ or families’ personal information with any government body except where Canadian law requires it.
