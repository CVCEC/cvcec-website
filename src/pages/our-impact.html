Where we work

A Vietnamese learning network across Canada

Many Vietnamese-Canadian families live far from a community class. We connect in-person classes, volunteer teachers and an online platform so children in different provinces can learn together.

Ontario

Toronto

Head office, the Viet Youth Readiness Hub, and a community Vietnamese class for children aged 4–6 launched in April 2026.

Prince Edward Island

31 children · ages 5–14

Vietnamese language and culture classes, supported by more than 20 parents who help with classes and community activities.

Manitoba

Online class

An online class in Manitoba learns together with students in Prince Edward Island, forming one learning community.

Across Canada

436 free lessons & activities

Trường Tiếng Việt Của Em, our free online school, is open to families anywhere, with lessons, games and worksheets.

Figures as of November 2026. Sources: VietnamPlus, 27 Oct 2026 and CVCEC records.
