Viet Youth Readiness Hub

A youth initiative launched in Toronto in April 2026 that helps Vietnamese-Canadian and newcomer youth build confidence, leadership and civic skills, and feel at home in Canadian schools and communities.

About VYRH

Ready for school, ready for community, ready to lead

The Viet Youth Readiness Hub (VYRH) is operated by the Canada Vietnam Cultural & Educational Council, a federal not-for-profit. It brings together newly arrived international students and Canadian-born youth of Vietnamese background, and is open to all families.

Through orientation sessions, mentoring, volunteering and youth-led projects, members learn how Canadian schools and civic life work, practise leadership in real settings and stay connected to Vietnamese language and culture.

What members do

Real experiences in Canadian community life

School readiness

Back-to-school orientation and peer support for newcomer students, including an orientation and Vietnamese language celebration in August 2026.

Civic learning

Members take part in local youth councils, including the Don Valley North Youth Council, and learn how decisions are made in their community.

Volunteering

Youth volunteer at community events and festivals, such as the Asialicious Carnival in September 2026.

Career readiness

Members met employers and public services such as Toronto Fire Services at the Davenport Job Fair in April 2026.

Recognition

Selected for Leading Urban Change at the University of Toronto

CVCEC’s project Bridging Heritage Language and Cultural Inclusion in Urban Education, led by Founder and President Trang Phan, was one of 16 initiatives selected for the 2026 cohort of the Leading Urban Change program at the University of Toronto’s School of Cities. Trang Phan completed the program in June 2026.

The project is developing a policy-aligned pilot model to bring Vietnamese language learning, cultural inclusion and youth civic engagement into urban public schools, starting in Toronto. VYRH is part of this work.
