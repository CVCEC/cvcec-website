Our story

From five children in a borrowed office to an official CVCEC branch

In October 2021, Ms. Hoàng Minh Hà (Ms. Hanna) opened a free Vietnamese class for about five children in a borrowed office in downtown Charlottetown. The pandemic paused the class, and in October 2024 it reopened with seven children.

Within two years the class had grown into three groups, and on June 1, 2026 it joined CVCEC as its PEI Branch. Ms. Hanna became CVCEC’s Vietnamese Language & Academic Director, and the PEI teaching approach now guides the whole Trường Tiếng Việt Của Em curriculum.

The guiding principle is “Tiên học lễ, hậu học văn”: learn respect first, then letters. Children learn to read and write Vietnamese together with kindness, gratitude to parents and grandparents, and respect for others and for the law. They grow up proud to be both Canadian and Vietnamese.

Classes

When and where we learn

In person · Charlottetown

Saturdays, 10:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. at Sport PEI, Charlottetown. No classes in July and August.

Two main teachers and three assistants each Saturday. 24 children attend.

Online · Summerside and beyond

Mondays, Tuesdays and Fridays, 8:00 to 9:00 p.m., all year. Three one-hour sessions for new, basic and advanced learners.

One or two teachers per session, with our Manitoba teacher leading online classes. 7 children from Summerside attend.

What children learn

Language, culture and character

Vietnamese language

Listening, speaking, reading and writing, following one shared curriculum with our online school.

History, culture and traditions

Lunar New Year with bánh chưng, the Mid-Autumn Festival, áo dài, stories from Vietnamese history and family customs.

Values

Respect, kindness, gratitude and respect for the law. Older students help younger ones read.
