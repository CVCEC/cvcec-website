Mentoring Program for Professional Development of Educators

Founded by Phan Thị Quỳnh Trang in 2021, the program helps teachers grow their skills, their professional identity and their network through mentoring. Today it is a CVCEC initiative.

How it works

Mentoring circles, not lectures

Mentors and mentees work in small groups where teachers with similar goals learn from each other. Mentors are educators with at least five years of experience; mentees include student teachers, new graduates and teachers changing careers. No experience is required to join as a mentee.

Earlier seasons were run with education technology partners ClassIn and ClassPoint, giving teachers hands-on practice with online and interactive teaching.

  • Professional CV, web portfolio and personal brand for every mentee
  • Interactive lessons and e-learning resources
  • Teaching demos with feedback from experts
  • Webinars and workshops, with mentees as assistants and mentors as guest speakers
  • Personal professional development plans with written feedback

  1. Stage 1

    Warm up

    CV, web portfolio, profile photo, interactive lessons and content planning.

  2. Stage 2

    Speed up

    Offline and hybrid workshops, livestreams and a shared bank of lessons.

  3. Stage 3

    Develop

    A library of model lessons, teacher meet-ups and monthly webinars.

  4. Stage 4

    Finish line

    A closing event where mentees share their work and reflections.
